Our story

The Story Behind Slice Story

Sometimes, the most meaningful stories begin in the most unexpected places.

By Clark, Founder

01Where it began

The story of Slice Story began at the Alberta Children's Hospital, during a chapter of our lives we never imagined experiencing.

Among the many memories we carry from our time there, Wednesday evenings hold a special place in our hearts. Every week, Kids Cancer Care Pizza Night at the Children's Hospital brought a little warmth and joy to the unit where our family spent so much time. Funded and run by Kids Cancer Care, this special program offered children and their families a little joy and comfort through something as simple as a pizza night.

For families facing the challenges of childhood cancer, those Wednesday evenings offered a welcome moment of comfort amid the hospital routine.

That's when we began to see pizza as more than just food.

Sometimes, something as simple as a pizza night can brighten someone's day, bring a little comfort, and create memories that stay with us long after the moment has passed.

02A love for craft

It also brought back memories of my travels through Italy, where I fell in love with the simplicity and craftsmanship of authentic Italian pizza. I remember the aroma of freshly baked dough, the delicate texture of a light, airy crust, and how a few carefully chosen ingredients could create such incredible flavour.

Fresh mozzarella, rich tomatoes, fragrant basil, and the unmistakable character of pizza baked in a hot oven. Every bite was a reminder of just how special a well-made pizza could be.

03The idea

Over time, those two experiences came together and sparked an idea. What if we could combine the authentic Italian pizza I had come to love with the warmth and kindness we experienced at the hospital? What if great pizza could travel beyond the walls of a traditional restaurant and become part of the places where people gather, celebrate, and connect?

We envisioned a mobile pizzeria that could bring those experiences together, serving exceptional pizza while giving back to a cause close to our hearts.

That is the beginning of Slice Story Pizzeria.

04Today

Today, we're bringing that vision to life in Calgary through handcrafted, flame-baked Neapolitan-style pizzas, made with thoughtfully selected ingredients, traditional inspiration, and a genuine passion for sharing great food.

But our story has always been about more than making great pizza.

Our journey began in a place where kindness, compassion, and community made a lasting difference in our lives. We want to carry that same spirit forward.

$1

That's why $1 from every pizza sold goes toward supporting local families with children facing cancer.

Because we believe even the simplest things can make a meaningful difference.

Every slice tells a story. Ours began with a Wednesday Pizza Night, a community that showed us the power of kindness, and a belief that sharing a simple meal can bring people closer together.
Clark Founder, Slice Story Pizzeria
